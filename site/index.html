Marswalk Ops
Plans a safe 6-hour, two-astronaut Marswalk (EVA, a walk outside in spacesuits) in Jezero Crater: it picks the science stops, finds the route over real NASA terrain, schedules the work and measures the risk. Try: press HUD mode to watch the walk play back, or add a stop in the Plan panel and watch it re-plan.
